Trust & Privacy

This page is maintained by Initial Air Pty Ltd to answer common questions about how we handle information you share through this website. It is editable content from the business owner and is not an independent certification or audit.

Who runs this site

Initial Air Pty Ltd, an HVAC contractor based at 7 Daisy St, Revesby NSW 2212. Contact: 0414 881 324.

What we collect

When you submit the quote form we collect your name, email, phone number, suburb (optional), the type of service you're after, the property type (optional), and any message you include. We collect this only to respond to your quote request.

We don't run analytics trackers or advertising pixels on this site.

Who can see your submission

Quote submissions are stored in a private database and are visible only to Initial Air staff who handle quotes. The public website cannot read submissions back — the form is write-only for visitors.

How it's protected

The site is served over HTTPS. The database enforces row-level security so the public role can only insert a new quote request and cannot read, edit, or delete existing records. Submissions are validated server-side (required fields, email format, length limits) before they are accepted.

These are platform-level controls provided by our hosting and database stack. They are not a substitute for a formal security certification.

Retention and deletion

We keep quote requests for as long as needed to follow up and for our standard business records. If you'd like your information removed, contact us at the phone number above and we'll delete it.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security problem with this site, please call 0414 881 324 so we can address it.
